\begin{abstract}\noindent
We carry the chart results back through the geometric blow-up to the physical variables,
completing Path~A's leading-order-with-prefactor analysis. The fold-weighted blow-up
$r=\ep_2^{1/3}R,\ y=\ep_2^{2/3}Y,\ t=\ep_2^{-1/3}T$ maps the physical SDE \emph{exactly}
onto the inner Riccati with effective noise $\eta=\sigma/\sqrt{\ep_2}$ (an It\^o change of
variables, no anomaly). Blowing down the chart conclusions then gives, in the original
variables: the critical-noise law
$\sigma_*^A=2\sqrt\pi\,\sqrt{\ep_2}\,G$, the inner exit (peel-off) measure
$\TW_\beta$ with $\beta=4\ep_2/\sigma^2$ at physical scales
$(r,y)\sim(\ep_2^{1/3},\ep_2^{2/3})$, the early-escape probability $1-F_\beta(0)$, and the
early-escape rate $e^{-c\,\ep_2/\sigma^2}$ with $c=5.4439\ldots$ The geometry factor $G$ is
$\sqrt{ac/b}$ frozen ($\alpha=2$) or $\sqrt{\langle a\rangle\langle c\rangle/\langle
b\rangle}$ averaged ($\alpha=1$). The noise collapse in $\sigma/\sqrt{\ep_2}$ is verified
in the physical variables. This assembles the six companion notes into one statement.
\end{abstract}

\section{The blow-up and the noise covariance}

The JKK normal form near the folded-cycle fold is, in physical variables,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:phys}
  \dd r=\bigl(b(\theta)r^2-a(\theta)y\bigr)\dd t+\sigma\,\dd W_t,\qquad
  \dd y=-\ep_2\,c(\theta)\,\dd t,\qquad \dot\theta=\ep_1,
\end{equation}
with $a,b,c>0$ smooth $1$-periodic and $0<\ep_2\ll\ep_1\ll1$, $\ep_1=\ep^\alpha$,
$\ep_2=\ep^3$. The Krupa--Szmolyan fold blow-up is the quasi-homogeneous (weights
$(1,2,3)$ on $(r,y,\ep_2)$) map covered by charts $K_1$ (entry), $K_2$ (rescaling), $K_3$
(exit). The central chart $K_2$ is $\rho=\ep_2^{1/3}$:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:K2}
  r=\ep_2^{1/3}R,\qquad y=\ep_2^{2/3}Y,\qquad T=\ep_2^{1/3}t .
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[exact noise covariance]\label{prop:noise}
Under \eqref{eq:K2}, \eqref{eq:phys} becomes the inner Riccati
\[
  \dd R=\bigl(b R^2-a Y\bigr)\dd T+\eta\,\dd B_T,\qquad \dd Y=-c\,\dd T,
  \qquad \boxed{\ \eta=\sigma/\sqrt{\ep_2}\ } ,
\]
exactly (an It\^o change of variables on the diffeomorphism \eqref{eq:K2}; the blow-up
introduces no It\^o correction because it is a smooth coordinate change). After the linear
rescaling absorbing $a,b,c$ this is the canonical $\dd R=(R^2-Y)\dd T+\eta\,\dd B$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
$\dd r=\ep_2^{1/3}\dd R$; the drift $(br^2-ay)\dd t=\ep_2^{2/3}(bR^2-aY)\cdot
\ep_2^{-1/3}\dd T=\ep_2^{1/3}(bR^2-aY)\dd T$; and the noise
$\sigma\,\dd W_t=\sigma(\dd t)^{1/2}\hat Z=\sigma(\ep_2^{-1/3}\dd T)^{1/2}\hat
Z=\sigma\,\ep_2^{-1/6}\dd B_T$. Dividing $\dd r=\ep_2^{1/3}\dd R$ through by $\ep_2^{1/3}$:
$\dd R=(bR^2-aY)\dd T+\sigma\,\ep_2^{-1/2}\dd B_T$. Similarly $\ep_2^{2/3}\dd Y=-\ep_2
c\,\ep_2^{-1/3}\dd T$ gives $\dd Y=-c\,\dd T$.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[scales]
The chart inner scales $R\sim\eta^{2/3}$, $Y\sim\eta^{4/3}$ blow down, via \eqref{eq:K2}
and $\eta=\sigma/\sqrt{\ep_2}$, to the \emph{physical} noise-induced peel-off scales
\[
  r_{\rm peel}\sim\ep_2^{1/3}\eta^{2/3}=\sigma^{2/3},\qquad
  y_{\rm peel}\sim\ep_2^{2/3}\eta^{4/3}=\sigma^{4/3},
\]
independent of $\ep_2$ --- the noise-induced jump scales --- while the typical (canard)
escape sits at $y=\ep_2^{2/3}a_1$.
\end{remark}

\section{The blow-down theorem}

Blowing down the chart conclusions (the reduction, the shooting characterisation, the
instanton rate, and the composition propagation, all $\theta$-uniform) through
\eqref{eq:K2} and Proposition~\ref{prop:noise} gives the physical statement.

\begin{theorem}[Path~A, physical variables]\label{thm:main}
For \eqref{eq:phys}, modulo the per-chart Berglund--Gentz tubes and the JKK deterministic
backbone:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[\textup{(i)}] \textup{(critical noise)} escape across the fold becomes likely at
\[
  \boxed{\ \sigma_*^A=2\sqrt\pi\,\sqrt{\ep_2}\;G\ },\qquad
  G=\begin{cases}\sqrt{a(\theta_*)c(\theta_*)/b(\theta_*)}, & \alpha=2\ (\text{frozen }\theta),\\[2pt]
     \sqrt{\langle a\rangle\langle c\rangle/\langle b\rangle}, & \alpha=1\ (\text{averaged}),\end{cases}
\]
uniformly in $\theta$;
\item[\textup{(ii)}] \textup{(inner exit measure)} the peel-off level is
$y_{\rm peel}=\ep_2^{2/3}\,Y_{\rm node}$ with $Y_{\rm node}\eqd\TW_\beta$,
$\beta=4\ep_2/\sigma^2$; equivalently the early-escape probability is
$\mathbb P_{\rm early}=1-F_{\beta}(0)$;
\item[\textup{(iii)}] \textup{(early-escape rate)} as $\sigma/\sqrt{\ep_2}\to0$,
$\mathbb P_{\rm early}=\exp\!\bigl(-c\,\ep_2/\sigma^2\,(1+o(1))\bigr)$, $c=5.4439\ldots$
\end{enumerate}
All three depend on $(\sigma,\ep_2)$ only through $\eta=\sigma/\sqrt{\ep_2}$ at leading
order.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By Proposition~\ref{prop:noise} the $K_2$ dynamics is the canonical inner Riccati with
$\eta=\sigma/\sqrt{\ep_2}$. (ii) is the reduction theorem ($Y_{\rm node}\eqd\TW_\beta$,
$\beta=4/\eta^2=4\ep_2/\sigma^2$) blown down by $y=\ep_2^{2/3}Y$; (iii) is the instanton
rate $e^{-c/\eta^2}=e^{-c\ep_2/\sigma^2}$. For (i): escape is order-one when the integrated
inner hazard $H=\eta^2/4\pi$ is order one, i.e.\ $\eta_*=2\sqrt\pi$, so
$\sigma_*=2\sqrt\pi\sqrt{\ep_2}$ in the canonical chart; restoring $a,b,c$ (the linear
rescaling that canonicalises $bR^2-aY$ with sweep rate $c$) multiplies by the geometry
factor $G$. The composition-propagation theorem carries these inner laws through the entry
($K_1$) and exit ($K_3$) charts with $\theta$-uniform, sub-dominant Gaussian corrections
(the fold renewal annihilating the entry deviation); the deterministic geometry is JKK
Theorem~3.2, whose $\alpha$-dichotomy (frozen vs.\ averaged $\theta$) gives the two cases
of $G$. Every estimate is $\theta$-uniform by the $\theta$-uniformity lemma.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Verification of the collapse.} Simulating the \emph{physical} system
\eqref{eq:phys} ($a=b=c=1$) through the fold and rescaling $Y=y_{\rm peel}/\ep_2^{2/3}$,
the early-escape probability and the peel-off law depend only on $\eta=\sigma/\sqrt{\ep_2}$:
\[
  \begin{array}{lll}
   \eta=1.5: & (\ep_2,\sigma)=(0.05,0.335):\ \mathbb P_{\rm early}=0.117,\ \langle Y\rangle=-1.20 & (0.20,0.671):\ 0.113,\ -1.21\\
   \eta=1.0: & (\ep_2,\sigma)=(0.05,0.224):\ \mathbb P_{\rm early}=0.011,\ \langle Y\rangle=-1.60 & (0.20,0.447):\ 0.010,\ -1.60
  \end{array}
\]
a clean collapse across a factor $4$ in $\ep_2$ (\texttt{blowdown.py}).

\section{Uniformity and the assembled statement}

\paragraph{Uniformity in $(\ep_1,\ep_2)$.} The $\ep_2$-dependence enters only through the
blow-up radius $\rho=\ep_2^{1/3}$ and the chart noise $\eta=\sigma/\sqrt{\ep_2}$
(Proposition~\ref{prop:noise}), so the chart estimates---$\theta$-uniform and
$\ep_2$-uniform once written in $\eta$---transfer verbatim. The $\ep_1=\ep^\alpha$
dependence enters only through the angular drift $\dot\theta=\ep_1$: for $\alpha=2$ the
angle is frozen on the passage timescale and $G$ is local; for $\alpha=1$ the angle is
fast and Khasminskii averaging replaces $a,b,c$ by $\langle a\rangle,\langle
b\rangle,\langle c\rangle$ with $O(1/\ep_1)$ corrections. This is exactly JKK Theorem~3.2's
deterministic $\alpha$-dichotomy, now carrying the noise.

\paragraph{The assembled Path~A.} Theorem~\ref{thm:main} is the capstone of the chain:
\emph{Cole--Hopf} (exact It\^o) $\Rightarrow$ \emph{reduction} to the stochastic Airy
operator $\Rightarrow$ \emph{shooting characterisation} ($Y_{\rm node}\eqd-\Lambda_0\eqd
\TW_\beta$, proved) $\Rightarrow$ \emph{instanton} rate $c=5.4439$ (proved) $\Rightarrow$
\emph{composition propagation} ($\theta$-uniform, fold renewal, proved) $\Rightarrow$
\emph{blow-down} (this note) to the physical critical-noise, exit-measure and rate laws.
The folded-limit-cycle canard escape is thereby placed in the Tracy--Widom / KPZ edge
universality class, in the original variables.

\section{Honest scope}

The blow-down is rigorous \emph{given} the chart results: Proposition~\ref{prop:noise} is
an exact change of variables, the scale blow-downs are algebra, and the assembly is the
composition theorem plus JKK's deterministic backbone. The remaining softness is inherited,
not introduced: the per-chart $K_1,K_3$ tubes are cited Berglund--Gentz (standard for
hyperbolic charts), and Channel~B is scaling-level. What this note adds is the final
desingularisation---turning the $O(1)$ chart statements into the physical-$(\sigma,\ep_2)$
theorem---which was the last named structural item of Path~A.
