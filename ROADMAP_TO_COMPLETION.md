## 2. What "100%" honestly means

Not "every conjecture proved" — open problems are the normal end-state of mathematics. **100% = a
complete, coherent, *demonstrated* account with the open program demarcated:** the proved spine proved
(fold = TW), the new results stated with evidence and tagged proved/numeric/conjectured, the loop to
the neurons closed, and the whole thing written as one self-contained work. The residual after that is
an *explicitly bounded* research program, not a deficiency.

## 3. The path: three campaigns, ordered to de-risk each other

### Campaign 1 — KEYSTONE: close the loop (→ fills D, E; validates A–F physically)
Sweep the electrical coupling g in the two-neuron FHR model; measure escape / MMO-timing statistics;
**demonstrate the ladder in the real model**: weak coupling → fold/TW escape (kurtosis ≈ 0); through
g_crit → cusp/Weber-TW (kurtosis flips negative), with the Δ(g)=2√(−2g/3) onset and the Bessel-level
structure. Then build the regime atlas (g × heterogeneity × delay) **labeled by singularity** (D), and
classify perturbation responses (coherence resonance, phase-flip) **by rung** (E).
→ Outcome: theory *demonstrated*, not just derived; D, E → ~85. The single most completing move.

### Campaign 2 — DEPTH CAPSTONE: nail the exact law (→ B→100, most of F)
Build the deterministic gateway properly (implicit Crank–Nicolson explosion-PDE for the full CDF) and
the **parabolic-cylinder / Painlevé-IV object** (Fredholm determinant). The tails already pin both ends
(α_L=5, α_R=3); identify the *full* cusp law to high numerical precision and state the PIV-transcendent
conjecture precisely with its evidence.
→ Outcome: B → 100; F → ~85; the core is as closed as numerics allow, with a sharp conjecture handed to
the proof program.

### Campaign 3 — CLOSER: the definitive paper (→ write-up; coherence)
Consolidate into one rigorous account — *The catastrophe ladder of noise-induced escape*: fold (proved
TW), cusp (Weber-TW), the ladder (Bessel-1/(q+2) skeletons, exact tails 2q+1 / 3q⁄2), the
Riccati-explosion characterization, the **neuron demonstration**, and the demarcated open program.
Tagged throughout.
→ Outcome: a coherent, defensible whole → ~95–100; residual = the explicitly-open proof program.

## 4. Why this order

The loop closure (1) anchors everything and is the project's original purpose — do it first so the
paper has a *demonstrated* payoff and the exact-law work has a physical home. The exact law (2) is the
intellectual capstone. The paper (3) can only be written once 1 and 2 are in hand.

## 5. The single BIG step to start

**Close the loop (Campaign 1).** It is the most completing single move: it validates the entire
abstract edifice in the concrete model, fills the two lowest rungs, and answers the question every
reader will ask. Everything else becomes easier — and more meaningful — once the theory is shown to
live in the neurons.
