## Direction 1 — Three-timescale / folded-limit-cycle GSPT: **young, but owned by Kuehn's group**

**Verified facts:**
- The defining paper — *Geometric Blow-Up for Folded Limit Cycle Manifolds in Three
  Time-Scale Systems* — is by **Jelbart, Kuehn & Kuntz**, *J. Nonlinear Sci.* 34:17
  (2024) ([arXiv:2208.01361](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.01361)). So the area is an
  **active programme of Kuehn's own group** — i.e. Popović's direct collaborators.
- The "oscillatory / limit-cycle GSPT is **less developed**, particularly in the
  non-normally-hyperbolic regime" claim is real and stated by the leaders themselves
  (their abstract). So the subfield genuinely has room — this part is **not** deflated.
- I found **no published stochastic / noise-driven version** of folded-limit-cycle
  blow-up (targeted search returned nothing; "if it exists, very recent / not indexed").
  A noisy folded-limit-cycle / three-timescale problem is the natural intersection of
  your GSPT **and** your FW/large-deviations toolkit — a concrete, unfilled-looking
  opening.

**What I could NOT verify:** whether Jelbart/Kuehn/Kuntz already flag (or are working)
the stochastic extension — their conclusion/future-work was beyond the extracted text.
Given Berglund–Gentz–**Kuehn** already do stochastic slow-fast, the noisy extension is
an *obvious* next step for exactly this group, so the prior that they're on it is real.

**Verdict (1):** genuinely young subfield (verified) + a concrete toolkit-matched
opening (stochastic version, no publication found) — **the strongest surviving lead**,
materially better than the three deflated bets because the openness here is the
*leaders' own statement* plus a *missing piece*, not my outside guess. **But "open for
you" hinges on Kuehn's unpublished pipeline, which I cannot see.** That single question
is decisive — and Popović, as a Kuehn collaborator, is exactly the person who knows.

## Direction 2 — Sharp SISR: **partially deflated (established + occupied)**

**Verified facts:**
- The analytical theory is **established at leading order**: Muratov–Vanden-Eijnden–E,
  *Self-induced stochastic resonance in excitable systems*, Physica D (2005) — the
  LDT / timescale-matching theory, with the period's dependence on noise and timescale
  ratio. DeVille–Vanden-Eijnden–Muratov is the companion.
- It has been **actively worked for ~20 years**, notably by **Yamakou** (many papers,
  2017–2025: conditions, control, networks, weak-noise regimes).
- The 2025 paper ([arXiv:2510.22848](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.22848), Savaliya–
  Yamakou) is a **physics-informed ML surrogate**, not an analytical closure. Its very
  existence signals that quantitative SISR in **realistic (finite-ε, finite-noise)**
  regimes is *not* well-captured by the existing sharp analytics — so an analytical gap
  plausibly exists.

**Verdict (2):** there likely *is* an open sharp/finite-ε gap — but it is a
**sharpening on top of a 20-year-established, actively-occupied theory** (Muratov–VE +
Yamakou). That is structurally the **c₀ situation**: real, but prone to landing
**high-A2 (incremental)** rather than A1, and at genuine risk of being more-done than
it looks. **Downgrade relative to (1).**
