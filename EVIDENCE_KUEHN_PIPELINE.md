# Evidence check: is Kuehn's group doing the stochastic folded limit cycle "as we speak"?

*Coverage: author searches on Kuntz / Kuehn / Engel (2024–2026); Christian Kuehn's
**dblp** publication list (current to 2025-08-23). Hard limits: I cannot see
unpublished/in-progress work, nor preprints from ~Sep 2025–Jun 2026 not yet indexed
or surfaced by search.*

---

## Verdict: no visible evidence they're doing *this specific thing*; mild signals in your favour; one real blind spot.

### Evidence they are **not** currently on it
1. **The natural extender has left the topic.** Sara-Viola Kuntz — co-author of the
   folded-limit-cycle blow-up and the obvious person to add noise to her own paper —
   has pivoted to **machine learning / neural ODEs** in 2024–25: *Morse functions &
   neural ODEs* (CoRR 2405.09351), *A Dynamical Systems Perspective on Neural Networks*
   (2507.05164), *Memory Capacity of Neural DDEs* (2505.07244). Her visible pipeline is
   ML, not stochastic dynamics.
2. **Kuehn's recent blow-up work is deterministic.** 2024–25: Galerkin fast-slow PDEs
   with Engel (SIADS 2024); the folded-limit-cycle paper itself (JNS 2024) is the
   deterministic one. **No stochastic-blow-up-of-a-singularity paper in his list.**
3. **Engel** (best-positioned for stochastic blow-up — he has both backgrounds) is
   doing deterministic PDE blow-up + stochastic-*ML*, not stochastic blow-up of folds.

### Evidence for caution (the area is adjacent-active)
- **Ahsan, Dankowicz, Kuehn (SIADS 2025):** *Adjoint-Based Projections for Quantifying
  Statistical Covariance near **Stochastically Perturbed Limit Cycles and Tori**.*
  So Kuehn **is** working on noise + limit cycles — but via the **direct / adjoint-
  covariance** method, **not** blow-up of the folded singularity. Two implications:
  (i) the noisy-limit-cycle area is on his radar; (ii) the **blow-up approach is
  precisely the un-done one** — your method would be the geometric alternative to
  their direct method (the same "blow-up vs direct estimates" contrast, one level up).

### The blind spots (honest)
- dblp is current only to **2025-08-23** → roughly **Sep 2025–Jun 2026 is uncovered**
  here; the 2025–26 web searches didn't surface a stochastic-folded-limit-cycle paper
  either, but that coverage isn't exhaustive.
- **Unpublished / just-started work is invisible** to any search, by definition.

## Net

The published/preprint record shows **no sign** Kuehn's group is doing the stochastic
folded-limit-cycle-via-blow-up, and the obvious next-mover (Kuntz) has moved to ML —
**the first piece of evidence in your favour** in this whole search. But Kuehn is
circling noisy limit cycles by the *direct* method, and "right now / unpublished" is
unknowable from the record. So this **lowers the pipeline risk without eliminating
it.** The definitive check is still one email to Popović — but now you'd send it with
evidence behind it, not a hunch:

> *"As far as I can tell from the record, the stochastic / blow-up treatment of the
> folded limit cycle (Jelbart–Kuehn–Kuntz 2024) hasn't been done — Kuntz seems to be on
> ML now, and the Ahsan–Dankowicz–Kuehn 2025 noisy-limit-cycle work uses adjoint/
> covariance methods rather than blow-up. Is anyone in Christian's group on the blow-up
> version, or is it free? It's my blow-up + Freidlin–Wentzell toolkit."*
